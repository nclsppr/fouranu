## My choice for a terrace and 30 cm pizzas

**I would choose the Koda 2 for outdoor gas cooking without the weight of the large ovens.** Its claimed 35 cm gives room around a 30 cm pizza; its 16 kg already deserves a planned location. I would rule it out if the oven must come inside after every meal or cook indoors.

That preference concerns size, weight and fuel. The bakes and readings below remain those of the cited authors, in their weather and settings. To place the choice within the range, see [which Ooni oven to choose](/en/ooni/which-ooni-oven/).

## The table matters as much as the stated 35 cm

The Koda 2 runs on propane and is intended for outdoor use. It accepts pizzas
up to 35 cm, weighs 16 kg and uses a 15 mm stone. Ooni publishes dimensions of
545 × 472 × 333 mm.

The inch measurements shown on the same page do not all match the metric
values. A few centimetres are enough for an oven to overhang a table. Measure
the location with a margin and confirm its depth before purchase.

## Three timers and no honest average

Three published videos exceed 400°C on the stone. To monitor this at home, the
useful reference remains an [infrared thermometer](/en/pizza-accessories/infrared-thermometer-guide/).
The videos do not use the same protocol.

| Published conditions | Observed reading | What it can tell us |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rain, air at around 13°C | Over 427°C after 20 minutes, around 482°C after 30 minutes | Mild, damp weather does not prevent high heat |
| Air at 2°C, oven initially covered with snow | 400°C at 25 minutes, plateau near 450°C at 35 minutes | Cold lengthens the session without preventing heating |
| Propane at full power | Over 400°C at around 12 minutes, about 435°C at 16 min 54 sec | A fast reading, but isolated and obtained differently |

Adding these times and dividing them would produce nothing useful. Outdoor
temperatures, measurement points and pacing differ. The oven can exceed 400°C
in varied conditions. The time at home will depend on the weather and the area
of the stone being measured.

## During the bake, stay close to the peel

One documented pizza bakes in about a minute after a stated launch at around
425°C. Another session, conducted in winter, is closer to two minutes. The
first rotation comes after thirty seconds, followed by further turns at short
intervals.

The rear burner colours one side more strongly. You must turn more often than
with a flame wrapping around two or three sides. One sequence also shows that
the limited space at the back can obstruct a brush when the oven is hot. This
detail does not condemn the Koda 2, but it matters for maintenance.

## Who will find it simpler

The Koda 2 suits a household that cooks outdoors, wants to stay with gas and
seeks more room than a 30 cm stone. It is easier to place than the
[Koda 2 Pro](/en/ooni/koda-2-pro/) and weighs much less than the
[Koda 2 Max](/en/ooni/koda-2-max/).

We would rule it out for a balcony where rules or clearances remain uncertain,
for indoor use, or for baking two pizzas at once. If the ritual of wood is part
of the project, the [Karu 2](/en/ooni/karu-2/) answers a different desire.

## Before ordering

- Measure the depth of the table and leave room for the hose.
- Check safety clearances around and above the oven.
- Check the propane connection supplied for France.
- Leave enough room for a [peel](/en/pizza-accessories/pizza-peel-guide/) and
  for turning a 35 cm pizza.
- Decide where a 16 kg appliance will be stored between uses.

## Alternatives I would compare before paying

For a fixed installation and larger pizzas, the [Koda 2 Pro / Gozney Arc XL comparison](/en/pizza-ovens/koda-2-pro-or-arc-xl/) introduces another size and another brand. Do not go larger simply because the table allows it: ask what diameter you will actually prepare.

If cooking indoors is the constraint, the [Volt 2, Sage and Cuisinart comparison](/en/pizza-ovens/volt-2-sage-or-cuisinart/) becomes the right starting point. The Koda 2 remains an outdoor oven; changing fuel first changes the installation.

To keep it on your shortlist, verify the exact depth, clearances, French gas connection and suitable peel. The [complete budget](/en/pizza-ovens/pizza-oven-full-budget/) then helps compare ready-to-use equipment instead of the oven price alone.

## An oven to install, not take out of a cupboard

The Karu 2 Pro is not simply a widened Karu 2. At 28.4 kg, with a 40 cm pizza
opening, glass door and display, it needs a lasting position. It makes sense if
you want to cook with wood often while retaining the option to switch to gas.
For occasional outings, its mass and complexity are hard to justify.

The propane burner is sold separately. A bundle may group it with the oven,
but that does not create a different model. Keep that distinction in mind when
comparing prices.

## A 43 cm stone and nearly 30 kg

The published cooking surface is 43 cm wide. The oven measures 50 × 83 × 83 cm,
weighs 28.4 kg and uses a 15 mm stone. It adds a glass door, display and food
probe.

Ooni claims 500°C in 15 minutes and a wood-fired pizza in 60 seconds. Only one
video documents heating, and it uses gas rather than wood. These figures are
therefore not enough to establish a schedule that will apply at home.

## Changing fuel is not instantaneous

The observed conversion involves loosening two screws, removing the burner and
replacing the rear panel. The process looks accessible, but you will not switch
from gas to wood in the middle of a bake. Wait for the oven to cool before
handling the parts.

The integrated thermometer measures the air in the oven. To know the stone
temperature, you still need an
[infrared thermometer](/en/pizza-accessories/infrared-thermometer-guide/). An
impressive display reading does not guarantee that the cooking surface is ready
at its centre and edges.

## In this session, 30 to 40 minutes

In the available session, full temperature arrives after around 30 to 40
minutes. The stone starts at about 7°C and the gas remains at full power. This
result does not refute the fifteen-minute claim because the exact target,
climate and measurement point are not aligned.

After launching, the cook turns the flame down and then raises it to colour the
top. In this session, the New York-style pizzas require several rotations. This
movement tells us more about the oven than an isolated time. Even with plenty
of space, the heat of the stone and the heat of the flame still need to be
managed separately.

## Who it suits

The Karu 2 Pro suits a lasting outdoor installation on a solid table for
someone who genuinely wants to use wood, charcoal and gas. The 40 cm opening
also leaves more room to turn a small pizza or use a compatible dish.

Rule it out if you must store the oven after every use. At nearly 30 kg, its
portability becomes theoretical. The [Karu 2](/en/ooni/karu-2/) remains more
coherent if you want a simple format. If you mainly cook with gas, the
[Koda 2 Pro](/en/ooni/koda-2-pro/) avoids the parts associated with solid fuel.

## Check before purchase

- The depth and load capacity of the table.
- Clearances around the chimney and door.
- Total cost with gas burner, [peel](/en/pizza-accessories/pizza-peel-guide/),
  cover and infrared thermometer.
- The main fuel you will actually use.
- Space to clean and store the conversion parts.

## What remains to be checked

One video is not enough to measure performance or ease of learning. Wood-fired
preheating is notably absent.

We can therefore assess the footprint, conversion and observed techniques, but
not rank the Karu 2 Pro against gas ovens. Before buying, answer two practical
questions above all: do you have a fixed position, and will you genuinely cook
with fire often enough to justify this large format?

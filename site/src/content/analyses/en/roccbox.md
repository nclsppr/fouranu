## Our view in brief

The Roccbox remains interesting if you want a compact oven that mainly lives on
the terrace, with the option to add a wood burner. It was listed at €399.99 on
24 August 2026, with the gas burner and a peel stated as included in the box.

Do not buy it expecting a lightweight oven. Gozney gives a weight just below
20 kg. It can be loaded into a car and its legs fold, but the 13.5 kg Tread is
more coherent for frequent trips.

## Portable means transportable

With its legs open, the Roccbox is 473 mm high, 540 mm deep and 413 mm wide. The
opening measures 300 × 88 mm and the stone 310 × 340 mm. The format is therefore
aimed at individual 30 cm pizzas.

Its folding legs and removable burner make storage easier. Its weight changes
the meaning of “portable”, however: from the garage to the terrace, yes; carried
for any distance, much less so.

## What is included reduces the initial purchase

The Gozney product page lists the oven, removable propane burner, a
[peel](/en/pizza-accessories/pizza-peel-guide/) and the manual in the box. That
package is useful beside a bare oven because a launching peel is not an optional
accessory.

Still check the country-appropriate regulator, the cover and the surface on
which the oven will stand. The promotional price alone does not give you the
complete budget.

## Wood is an option, not an obligation

A removable wood burner is sold separately. This option distinguishes the
Roccbox from the propane-only Tread. It also adds wood storage, fire management
and ash removal.

If you already know you will always use gas, do not choose the Roccbox solely
for this possibility. Compare the price, weight and transport arrangements
instead.

## Roccbox or Tread

The Tread costs €499.99 and weighs 13.5 kg. It adds carrying handles and an
outdoor ecosystem. The Roccbox weighs about 6 kg more, but it was cheaper when
we checked and includes a peel according to the product page.

We would take the Tread for weekends away, camping or frequent journeys. We
would keep the Roccbox for a compact terrace, a few trips by car and a genuine
desire to try wood.

## Who it is for, and when to avoid it

The Roccbox suits a first 30 cm gas oven with a compact, semi-permanent setup.
Avoid it if every kilogram matters, if you want large pizzas or if the oven will
have to be carried far from the car.
